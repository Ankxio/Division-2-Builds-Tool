# Division 2 Builds Tool

A simple tool I built for **Tom Clancy's The Division 2** to make theorycrafting and sharing builds a lot easier. 

👉 **Check it out here:** [https://ankxio.github.io/Division-2-Builds-Tool/](https://ankxio.github.io/Division-2-Builds-Tool/)

---

## What it does

- **Build Creator**: Pick your weapons, gear sets, brand sets, attributes, and mods for every single slot.
- **Live Stats**: See your damage, CHC, CHD, skill tiers, and defense update automatically as you build.
- **Easy Sharing**: Share your loadout with friends or squadmates using shareable links or screenshots.
- **Works on Mobile**: Quick and clean layout whether you're on PC or your phone.

---

## How to use it

1. Choose your weapons and gear items.
2. Select your core stats, minor attributes, and set bonuses.
3. Check the stats panel on the side to tweak your setup.
4. Copy the link or grab a screenshot to share it!
